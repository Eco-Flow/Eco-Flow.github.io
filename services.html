Services

Our remit is to grow a Nextflow community within agri-ecology research. Here's how we help — we aim to reply to every enquiry within two working days.

Nextflow training

Hands-on training roughly every six months, with limited places at host institutions. We can also bring custom basic or advanced training to your institute.

Want to start now? Two free courses to work through:

Nextflow for Ecology & Evolutionary Biology Our own free, beginner-friendly course, built for ecologists and evolutionary biologists new to pipelines.
The official Nextflow training The broader, general-purpose Nextflow course from Seqera — once you want to go further.
