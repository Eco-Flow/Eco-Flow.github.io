On **Wednesday 27 May 2026**, Chris from Eco-Flow spoke at the **[Mini-Symposium on Evolutionary Genomics](https://bio.kuleuven.be/news/mini-symposium-on-evolutionary-genomics)**, hosted by the Department of Biology at **KU Leuven**.

### The talk

*"Extensive genome rearrangements in the haplodiploids: a novel pipeline for syntenic break"* looked at how chromosomes have been reshuffled across the haplodiploid insects, and how to detect and quantify those rearrangements at scale with **[Eco-Flow/synteny]({{ '/pipelines/synteny/' | relative_url }})**, our Nextflow pipeline built to nf-core standards.

The visit was also a chance to introduce **Nextflow** and the **nf-core** community to evolutionary genomicists who hadn't used them before.

### The programme

The symposium also included talks from **Emilia Santos** (University of Cambridge) on the genomic and developmental basis of colour-pattern evolution, and **Sozos Michaelides** (Concordia University) on lake trout adaptations in a large postglacial lake.

Thank you to **Tom Wenseleers** for the invitation.
