If you are interested in joining the **[2026 nf-core Hackathon](https://nf-co.re/events/2026/hackathon-march-2026)** in London. This session aims to give you a afternoon of hackathon learning, so you can contribute to the nf-core ecosystem. 

# Topics

- Basics of nf-core (nf-core tools)
- Pull Requests
- Adding modules
- Updating software
- SeqeraAI support 

# Aim

We hope by the end of the day you may begin to be able to change nf-core repos in small but important ways. In this way, during the hackathon you can take these skills and immeidately contribute to nf-core.

# Prequisites

We assume you know the basics of Nextflow to be enrolled in this workshop. This means that you have already run through the training on hello nextflow -> https://training.nextflow.io/2.8.1/ and already had a quick look at "hello nf-core" -> https://training.nextflow.io/2.8.1/hello_nf-core/
