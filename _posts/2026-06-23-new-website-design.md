If the site looks a little different today — that's because it is. We've given the Eco-Flow website a complete redesign. And we're proud to admit that we took advantage of Claude to push this forward, with a lot of our input!

The old site did its job, but as the project has grown so has everything we want to share: more pipelines, more training, more events, and a growing community. It was time for a refresh that could keep up.

### What's new

- **A clearer homepage** that explains what Eco-Flow is at a glance, with our four guiding principles — building, testing, training and community — front and centre.
- **A proper pipelines section.** Every pipeline now has its own page, grouped by how far along it is (released, in development, or early stage), so you can see what's ready to use and what's coming.
- **Easier navigation** with a tidied-up menu and a layout that finally works properly on phones and tablets.
- **A fresher design** — cleaner typography, more space to breathe, and a green palette that suits a project rooted in ecology.

### Same content, easier to find

All our existing blog posts, event listings and pipeline documentation have made the move. If you had an old link bookmarked, it should still take you to the right place.

### Tell us what you think

This is very much a living site, and we'll keep improving it. If you spot something broken, have an idea, or just want to get involved, [drop us a line](mailto:ecoflow.ucl@gmail.com) or find us on [GitHub](https://github.com/Eco-Flow/).
