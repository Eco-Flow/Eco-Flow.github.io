# Services

Our remit is to assist in the development of a Nextflow community within the Agri-ecology reasearch field, specifically we aim to :

1. Provide Nextflow training (every six months, limited in places in host institutions).
2. Collaborate on building custom Nextflow pipelines (See : [our pipelines](pipelines.md))
3. Provide technical assistance (e.g. HPC setup, debugging, demo run).
4. Host hackathons and outreach events (e.g. the London nf-core hackathon, see our blog)

Please contact us if you need our help, we aim to return your email with 2 working days (ecoflow.ucl @ gmail.com)

If you would like a custom basic nextflow or nf-core training at you institute, please contact us. We are happy to either host one at your university (subject to our time constraints), or invite you to one of our existing/upcoming events.
